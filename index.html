How a piece moves through

The same sequence whether it's one bracket or a full production batch.

01

Treating / Cleaning & prep

Every seam is carfully cleaned and treated from any rust before it goes anywhere near paint.

02

Electrostatic coating

Powder is applied by electrostatic gun for even coverage, including corners and returns.

03

Cure, inspect & ship

Oven-cured, checked for finish and coverage, then packed for delivery or site install.
